# New Settings Section — Community & Feedback

## Purpose

Kloyya is being built alongside its users.

The Settings page should include a dedicated section where users can submit ideas, report problems, request features, and invite others to join the beta.

This section helps Kloyya continuously improve based on real customer feedback.

## Referral Rewards

During beta there are no monetary rewards.

Instead users receive:

- Early feature access
- Priority beta support
- Direct influence on the roadmap
- Exclusive founder updates
- Early access to new AI capabilities
- Recognition as an Early Supporter

Future rewards may include:

- Extended free subscription
- Premium features
- Workspace credits
- Community badges

# Design Principles

This section should feel personal and conversational.

Users should feel that:

- Their feedback is genuinely valued.
- They are helping build Kloyya.
- Every suggestion has the potential to shape the product.
- They are part of an exclusive early community, not just using another software application.

The goal is to create long-term relationships with early adopters while continuously improving Kloyya based on real user needs.
